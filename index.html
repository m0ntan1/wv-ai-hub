02Public Service Commission

Decision due Mar 6, 2027 Case 26-0075-E-CN
MARL 500 kV line (NextEra Energy Transmission MidAtlantic)

About 58.9 WV miles through Monongalia, Preston, Mineral, and Hampshire. On Aug 19 PSC Staff moved to make NextEra toll the case or have it dismissed. The Commission denied that on Sep 4, kept the Mar 6, 2027 decision date, and ordered NextEra to study using the existing Gore–Hampshire right-of-way instead. Staff witness Daniel Egnatoff recommended denial. The Jefferson County Commission, Hampshire, Mineral, and Harrison County Commissions, Friends of the Cheat, and the Hampshire County Farmland Protection Board weighed in. More than 2,000 protest signatures and letters were filed Aug–Nov. The hearing ran Oct 26–30 and Nov 2 and was live-streamed.

04Legislature & Policy

Law · 2025 HB 2014
Power Generation and Consumption Act

Creates High Impact Data Center (≥90 MW) certification and microgrid districts of up to 2,250 acres, and preempts county and city zoning. Host counties get 30% of the property-tax revenue. Signed by Gov. Patrick Morrisey on Apr 30, 2025.

WV Legislature →
Law · 2026 HB 4983
Certification rule + confidentiality

Approves rule 145 CSR 20 and keeps certification petitions confidential. The House passed it 78-16. Local-control bills HB 5620 and SB 658, and the water-notice bill HB 5590, all died.

Mountain State Spotlight →
Policy · Aug 11, 2026 Governor's plan
Responsible Data Center Development Plan

Seven principles over 20 years, run by the Data Economy Office (Director Chris Morris). It promises a Data Center Advisory Council on setbacks and noise. No roster has been published.

Plan (PDF) →
2027 session Reform push
Repeal or amend HB 2014

Democratic Chair Mike Pushkin, Del. Shawn Fluharty, the WV NAACP (moratorium resolution), and the WVDCAP coalition are pushing local control, water protections, and a fairer county revenue share.
