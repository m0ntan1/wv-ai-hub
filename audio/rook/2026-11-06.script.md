Doug Stalnaker lives in Frame, and he spoke to MetroNews on the record. He thinks the line mainly serves data centers in Virginia and Maryland, not West Virginia. His group gathered petition signatures outside. And he urged the protesters to stay civil.

The developers tell it differently. They say the line brings reliability upgrades, and about fourteen million dollars a year in property tax revenue.
