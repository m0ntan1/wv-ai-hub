The station says the ordinance regulates data centers in industrial districts. It says it sets application standards for electrical power demand, water consumption and other impacts.

The mayor said the city will stay in touch with other communities to see, quote, "what developing problems are." End quote.

Silicon Foundation said in July that its Warwood project would be limited to fifty megawatts. WTOV9 reported that.

My read: credit to the council for putting rules in writing and on the agenda. The question I'd bring to Tuesday is what they cover.
