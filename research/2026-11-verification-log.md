## Method (receipts)
1. **Inventory.** `verification/inventory.js` loads the page in a headless browser and lists every claim (hub stories, docket items, Who's Who entries, project cards, calendar events, standing meetings) with its source links.
2. **Link check.** All 150 unique source URLs were requested. Seven were dead (404) and were replaced with working sources. Pages that block automated clients (West Virginia Watch, FBI.gov, TV station sites, the PSC) or that render with JavaScript were read with Firecrawl instead. Those extracts are saved in `verification/extracts.py`.
3. **Fact matching.** For each item, the specific facts it asserts (names, numbers, dates, venues) were listed in `verification/verify_spec.py` and `verify_final.py`, and the script tested whether each fact appears in the item's source text. Every miss was checked by hand. Each one was either a wording difference, a source missing from the site (now linked), or a real error (corrected below).
4. **PSC items.** These were checked against the PSC Case Subscription docket digests in the curator's inbox. Those digests are the PSC's own notices, and each links to the filed document.
