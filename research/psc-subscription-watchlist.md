# PSC Case Subscription Watchlist (draft, Nov 3, 2026)

Cases at the Public Service Commission of West Virginia worth adding to your Case Subscription account so the daily docket digest covers the whole data-center and grid story, not just MARL and Fort Martin.

**How to add one:** go to psc.state.wv.us → **Case Information** → search the case number → open the case → **Subscribe to Case**. Manage everything at the PSC's *Case Subscription Management System* (the "Modify" link in your digest emails).

> ⚠ **Verify before relying on these.** The PSC website blocks this research environment, so I could not open most case pages directly. Case numbers come from PSC press releases, legal notices on wvlegals.com, and news reporting, linked below. Statuses are as of those sources, and some may be stale.
